Fix inverted sun rays, exposure time series, and UTCI rounding

Three critical fixes for MRT/UTCI calculation:

1. Inverted sun ray direction (exposure.py)
   - Changed ray_dir from -sun_vector to sun_vector (TO sun, not away)
   - Was causing discrete 3-5°C edge artifacts by inverting occlusion tests
   
2. Scalar fract_body_exp bug (mrt_calculator.py)
   - Pass HourlyContinuousCollection instead of single value to OutdoorSolarCal
   - Restores temporal variation in solar exposure

3. UTCI precision loss (utci_calculator.py)
   - Added round_output=False to pythermalcomfort.utci() calls
   - Prevents artificial quantization (15 → 2,300+ unique values/hour)

Also adjusted grid bounds (2m west, 1m other edges) to avoid mesh boundary 
precision issues, and made analysis date configurable.

Results: Discrete edges eliminated, smooth gradients, better validation alignment.
Added design rules document: rules/mrt_thermal_comfort_design_rules.mdc
